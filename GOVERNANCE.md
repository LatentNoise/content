# Governance

## Who decides

Content is developed and maintained by **Yann Orieult**, who is the sole
maintainer and the final decision-maker on every question: design, scope,
roadmap, releases and licensing.

There is no committee, no vote, no maintainer ladder, and no path to becoming a
co-maintainer. This is not a stage the project is passing through on its way to
a community model — it is the model.

## What that means in practice

- **Code contributions are not accepted.** See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Bug reports and feedback are welcome** through issues, and they do influence
  the roadmap — but opening an issue is not a commitment that anything will be
  built, fixed, or answered.
- **Releases** are made only by the maintainer. Anything published elsewhere
  under the name Content is not official.
- **The roadmap** ([docs/roadmap/](docs/roadmap/)) describes current intent, not
  a promise. It changes without notice.

## Forks

Forking is explicitly permitted by the AGPL and is the intended path if you want
Content to work differently. You need no permission and you do not need to tell
anyone.

A fork is an **independent project**. It must not present itself as official,
endorsed by, or affiliated with Content or its maintainer, and must not use the
project's name in a way that implies any of those. The licence grants you rights
over the code; it does not grant endorsement.

## Licensing

Content is published under [AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). The maintainer holds
sole copyright, which additionally allows the project to be offered under
separate commercial terms — see [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md).

Rights already granted under a published licence are irrevocable: nothing here
can take away what a previous release gave you.

## Changes to this model

This governance model may change at the maintainer's discretion. Any change
applies going forward only and cannot affect rights already granted under
licences already published.
